ShadowCart doesn't block you from buying anything. It just makes sure the version of you that clicked "Add to Cart" and the version of you checking out are the same person.

EXHIBIT B 🗂️

Every product you're considering, across every store, in one dark, calm, single interface — not fifteen abandoned carts.

EXHIBIT C ⏳

The cooling period

Each item enters a countdown before you can act on it. Default 48 hours. Yours to shorten, extend, or snooze.

EXHIBIT D 📩

When the timer runs out, get a quiet notification — and an optional email — asking one question: still want it?

Protocol

Four stages, start to finish.

The sequence is fixed — each stage only exists because the one before it happened.

01

Click "Add to Cart"

Anywhere on the web. ShadowCart intercepts it before the store's own cart ever sees it.

02

Tag the impulse

One tap: bored, stressed, need it, treating yourself. Skip if you're in a hurry.

03

Cool down

The item sits in your dashboard on a timer. No pressure, no expiring "deal," just time.

04

Decide, once

Timer's up — you get pinged. Buy it or drop it. Either way, it was your call, not a dark pattern's.

Deposition — Q&A

Common questions, answered plainly.

Straight answers, no legalese.

What is ShadowCart?

ShadowCart is a free, local-first browser extension that intercepts "Add to Cart" on any online store and holds the item in a private cooling-off queue — usually 48 hours — before you can check out.

Does it require an account?

No signup, email, or password. Everything is stored locally in your browser via IndexedDB, and nothing reaches a server unless you turn on optional email reminders.

How long is the cooling-off period?

48 hours by default — shorten, extend, or snooze it per item from your dashboard.

Which stores and browsers does it support?

Any online store, in Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers — it works at the browser level, not per storefront.

Is it free and open source?

Yes — free to use, source on GitHub, installable unpacked or built from source.
